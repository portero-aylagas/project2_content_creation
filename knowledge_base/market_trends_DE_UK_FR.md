# Market Trends — Germany, UK, France

## Germany

### Market Overview
Germany is the 3rd largest music market in Europe by streaming revenue.
Streaming accounts for 83% of music consumption. The German market has
distinctive characteristics: a strong domestic music preference (German-
language "Deutschpop" growing +28% YoY streams), Europe's #1 vinyl market
by units sold, and a powerful independent scene centred on Berlin, Hamburg,
Cologne, and Munich.

### Streaming Data
- Spotify: dominant platform with approximately 68% market share
- Apple Music: 18% market share — strongest with premium listeners
- Deezer: approximately 8% — declining but retains 25–40 age demographic
- YouTube Music: growing, particularly among 18–24 age group
- Believe Germany revenue: €111.3 million in FY 2024, up 0.4% YoY
  (digital trajectory returning positive after physical exit strategy)

### Genre Trends
- Indie pop and indie electronic: growing strongly, Berlin and Hamburg scenes
  driving European circuit bookings
- German-language hip-hop and rap: dominant domestic genre, consistently
  topping streaming charts
- Deutschpop (German-language pop): up 28% YoY streams
- Electronic/techno: Berlin remains global techno capital, strong streaming
  and live crossover for independent producers
- Post-punk influenced indie: growing following broader European trend

### Media Landscape
- Musikexpress: most influential indie print and digital publication
- tip Berlin: essential for Berlin-based artists
- ByteFM: independent radio, highly influential in indie and electronic community
- Rolling Stone DE: broader commercial coverage
- WDR Cosmo: public radio with indie programming
- BVMI (Bundesverband Musikindustrie): German music industry association,
  publishes quarterly streaming data

### Key Opportunities for Believe
- Independent electronic and techno artists from Berlin are growing on
  Spotify DE editorial playlists — Believe's local presence is an advantage
- German indie artists with bilingual (German/English) output are
  outperforming purely English acts in domestic editorial playlists
- Physical distribution exit complete — digital growth trajectory confirmed

## United Kingdom

### Market Overview
The UK is the 2nd largest music market in Europe. Streaming accounts for
91% of music consumption. The UK has Europe's strongest independent music
culture — 52% of chart acts are independent. The TikTok-to-streaming
discovery pipeline is more developed in the UK than any other European market.

### Streaming Data
- Spotify: dominant at approximately 72% market share
- Apple Music: 20% market share — crucial for industry credibility
  and press coverage
- Amazon Music: 6% and growing with Prime subscriber base
- YouTube: significant for music video consumption and independent
  artist discovery
- BPI (British Phonographic Industry) reports UK streaming grew
  consistently above European average in 2024

### Genre Trends
- Post-punk revival: continuing strongly following Wet Leg, Yard Act effect
  — labels actively seeking guitar bands
- Bedroom pop and lo-fi indie: growing on streaming (+34% YoY streams
  on Spotify UK indie playlists)
- Hard techno: Azyr (Blackpool) sold out 5,000-capacity Manchester venue
  in under 24 hours — fastest growing electronic subgenre in UK
- Indie folk: strong press coverage pipeline (BBC Introducing, MOJO, Uncut)
- Afrobeats/UK garage crossover: growing rapidly among 16–24 demographic

### Media Landscape
- NME: online-first, high traffic, covers emerging artists
- DIY Magazine: most respected indie tastemaker publication
- The Line of Best Fit: strong online, excellent for emerging artists
- MOJO / Uncut: older demographic, album-focused, 4-star review meaningful
- BBC Introducing: most important platform for emerging UK artists —
  direct pipeline to Radio 1 and BBC 6 Music
- Pitchfork UK coverage: significant for international breakout potential

### Key Opportunities for Believe
- Sentric (UK publishing) integration creates cross-sell opportunities:
  Sentric clients are natural Premium Solutions candidates
- UK independent market share growing year-on-year — more artists in
  Believe's target tier (50k–500k monthly listeners)
- BBC Introducing submissions highly accessible for independent artists —
  Believe's local team can support pitching strategy

## France

### Market Overview
France is the 4th largest music market in Europe. French-language music
accounts for 61% of top streaming charts — the highest domestic preference
ratio in Europe. Radio remains unusually powerful compared to other European
markets. Physical music (vinyl, CD) still accounts for 12% of revenue —
highest in Europe alongside Germany. Believe is strongest in France as its
home market, with €988.8M total revenues of which France is a significant
contributor.

### Streaming Data
- Spotify: 58% market share (lower than UK/Germany due to Deezer's
  home market advantage)
- Deezer: 28% market share — significantly higher than any other European
  market; Believe has a home-market advantage via direct Deezer relationships
- Apple Music: approximately 10%
- YouTube: very strong for discovery, especially 16–24 demographic
- SNEP (Syndicat National de l'Édition Phonographique): publishes
  monthly French streaming charts and market data

### Genre Trends
- French rap: dominant genre, Believe has strong roster (KeBlack, Heuss
  l'Enfoiré, Uzi, Carbonne — all charted top positions H1 2024)
- Indie électronique / synthpop: strong scenes in Lyon, Paris, Bordeaux —
  international crossover potential via La Blogothèque
- Indie française: artists embracing French cultural identity outperform
  those mimicking Anglo-American trends
- Afropop/Caribbean crossover: growing in Paris metropolitan market
- Rock français: niche but loyal audience, consistent streaming base

### Media Landscape
- Les Inrockuptibles: most prestigious indie publication in France
- Télérama: cultural institution, broad audience, album reviews carry weight
- La Blogothèque: internationally read, session videos have launched French
  artists globally — a single session can drive international discovery
- Radio Nova: influential independent radio, accessible to indie artists
- FIP (Radio France): curated, quality-focused, plays both international
  indie and French music
- France Inter: mainstream public radio — transformative if achieved
- FNAC: digital/physical retail newsletter reaches 180,000+ subscribers

### Key Opportunities for Believe
- Deezer's home market position is Believe's editorial relationship advantage —
  French artists distributed by Believe have preferential access
- La Blogothèque session pipeline: a session video can trigger international
  discovery for French indie artists — aligns with Believe's European expansion
- Avant-Garde label (launched 2023): rap, Afro, R&B — positioned to capitalise
  on French rap streaming dominance
