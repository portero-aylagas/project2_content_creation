# Believe — Monthly Market Intelligence Report Template

## Purpose
This template defines the structure, tone, and content requirements for Believe's
monthly market intelligence report. The AI system reads this file to understand
how to format and frame the report output.

---

## Report Identity

**Report name:** Believe Market Intelligence Report
**Frequency:** Monthly
**Audience:** Believe internal strategy team + Artist Solutions managers
**Markets covered:** Germany, UK, France (primary) + global highlights
**Tone:** Professional, data-driven, concise. No filler. Every sentence
should contain a fact, an insight, or an action recommendation.
**Length:** 800–1200 words. Executives read this in 5 minutes.

---

## Report Structure

The report must follow this exact section order every month.
Each section has a required length and a required format.

---

### SECTION 1 — Executive Summary
**Length:** 3–4 sentences maximum
**Format:** Plain prose, no bullets
**Content:** The 3 most important things that happened this month
across markets, competition, and platforms. What does it mean
for Believe specifically? What is the one action Believe should
consider based on this month's data?

**Example output:**
> Streaming revenue in Germany grew 8.2% YoY in October, driven by
> domestic indie pop releases outperforming international acts on
> Spotify DE editorial playlists. AWAL expanded its European A&R
> team with two new signings in Berlin, signalling increased competition
> for mid-tier independent artists in the German market. Believe should
> accelerate playlist pitching relationships with Spotify DE editorial
> ahead of Q1 release cycles.

---

### SECTION 2 — Market Trends
**Length:** 200–250 words
**Format:** One paragraph per market (Germany, UK, France),
followed by a Global Highlights paragraph
**Content per market:**
- Streaming growth or decline (% change, source)
- Dominant platform shifts
- Genre trends gaining or losing momentum
- Notable chart movements or viral moments
- Consumer behaviour changes

**Required data points:** At least 2 specific statistics per market
(e.g. "Spotify DE monthly active users grew 6% MoM")

**Global Highlights:** 2–3 sentences on macro trends affecting
all markets (e.g. paid subscription growth, AI music policy changes,
vinyl resurgence data)

---

### SECTION 3 — Platform Updates
**Length:** 150–200 words
**Format:** One bullet point per platform update, 2–3 sentences each
**Platforms to cover:** Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music,
TikTok (music-related only)
**Content:** Policy changes, new features for artists, editorial
restructuring, royalty model updates, playlist changes, algorithm
updates

**Flag system:**
- 🟢 Opportunity — update benefits independent artists
- 🟡 Monitor — update may affect Believe artists, watch closely
- 🔴 Risk — update negatively impacts independent artist revenue
  or visibility

---

### SECTION 4 — Competitor Intelligence
**Length:** 200–250 words
**Format:** One paragraph per competitor, 3–4 sentences
**Competitors to monitor:**
- DistroKid (DIY tier threat)
- AWAL / The Orchard (full-service tier direct competitor)
- Virgin Music Group / Warner Artist Services (major label indie arms)
- TuneCore (Believe's own DIY product — internal cannibalisation watch)

**Content per competitor:**
- Any pricing changes or new product launches
- New market entries or geographic expansion
- Notable artist signings or departures
- Marketing campaigns or positioning shifts
- Funding, acquisitions, or partnership news

**Competitive signal rating:**
- 🔴 High threat — requires immediate strategic response
- 🟡 Medium — monitor for 30 days
- 🟢 Low — noted, no action required

---

### SECTION 5 — Independent Artist Economy
**Length:** 150–200 words
**Format:** Plain prose with key statistics bolded
**Content:**
- Independent music market share movement
- Artist earnings data (streams, live, sync)
- Emerging revenue models (superfan tiers, direct-to-fan)
- Distribution volume trends (tracks uploaded per day to Spotify etc.)
- Any data on artist churn between platforms

---

### SECTION 6 — Market Opportunities
**Length:** 150–200 words
**Format:** 3 numbered opportunities, each with:
  1. Opportunity name (one line, bold)
  2. Evidence (1–2 sentences with data)
  3. Recommended Believe action (1 sentence)

**Content:** Based on all data in sections 2–5, what are the
3 most actionable opportunities for Believe this month?
These should be specific to Believe's business — not generic
industry observations.

**Example:**
> **1. German indie electronic breakout window**
> Spotify DE editorial added 14 new indie electronic tracks to
> editorial playlists in October, a 40% increase vs September.
> Believe should pitch its German electronic roster to Spotify DE
> editorial team in the next 14 days to capitalise on the editorial appetite.

---

### SECTION 7 — Data Sources Used
**Length:** Bullet list
**Format:** Source name + URL + date accessed
**Purpose:** Transparency and reproducibility. Every statistic in
the report must be traceable to a source listed here.

**Required sources to check monthly:**
- IFPI resources page: https://www.ifpi.org/resources/
- Music Business Worldwide: https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com
- MIDiA Research blog: https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog
- Spotify for Artists newsroom: https://artists.spotify.com/blog
- Hypebot: https://www.hypebot.com
- BPI (UK): https://www.bpi.co.uk/industry-insights
- BVMI (Germany): https://www.musikindustrie.de/en
- SNEP (France): https://www.snepmusique.com/en

## Formatting Rules

These rules apply to every section of every report:

1. **Always lead with data** — open every section with a statistic,
   not a general observation
2. **Name names** — reference specific platforms, artists, companies,
   and markets. Never say "a major platform" — say "Spotify"
3. **Every trend needs a number** — "growing" is not enough.
   "Growing 8.2% YoY" is acceptable
4. **One insight per paragraph** — do not combine multiple unrelated
   points in one paragraph
5. **Action orientation** — at least one actionable recommendation
   per section
6. **No filler phrases** — never use "it is worth noting", "it is
   interesting that", "as we can see"
7. **Consistent market order** — always Germany → UK → France
8. **Flag every competitor move** — use the 🔴🟡🟢 system in Section 4

## Quality Checklist

Before the report is finalised, verify:
- [ ] Executive summary is under 4 sentences
- [ ] Every market section has at least 2 statistics
- [ ] Every platform update has a 🟢🟡🔴 flag
- [ ] Every competitor section has a threat rating
- [ ] Section 6 has exactly 3 opportunities with recommended actions
- [ ] All sources are listed in Section 7
- [ ] Total word count is between 800 and 1200 words
- [ ] No section uses the word "interesting" or "notable"
