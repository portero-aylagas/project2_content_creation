# Believe Strategic Priorities

## FY 2025 Strategic Focus Areas

Based on Believe's FY 2024 results and public forward-looking statements,
the company has identified four core strategic priorities for 2025:

### 1. Value Optimisation
Move away from low-margin physical distribution contracts — particularly
in Germany — and focus investment on digital-first, high-margin artist
services. The company exited physical distribution contracts that were
"too heavily reliant on physical sales and merchandising" in Germany,
accepting short-term revenue impact for long-term margin improvement.

### 2. Controlled Investment and Selective Acquisitions
Rather than aggressive M&A, Believe is pursuing bolt-on acquisitions
in strategic markets — particularly publishing (Sentric model), regional
label imprints (PlayCode Japan, Krumulo Indonesia), and artist catalog
acquisitions. Cash allocation between artist advances and acquisitions
will remain disciplined.

### 3. Automation and Efficiency
The Be Odyssey technology programme aims to automate more of the
Central Platform's capabilities. TuneCore Accelerator demonstrates the
model: use technology to serve 450,000+ artists at scale with marketing
support previously only available to Premium Solutions clients.

### 4. Market Share Growth
Believe's organic growth forecast of +13% for FY 2025 assumes market
share gains in streaming — particularly in Europe and the Americas —
rather than relying on DSP price increases (which did not materialise
in FY 2024 as expected).

## Market Priorities by Region

### Germany — Stabilise and Digitalise
- Context: Revenue grew only 0.4% in FY 2024
- Strategy: complete exit from physical distribution contracts,
  accelerate digital Premium Solutions pipeline
- Opportunity: Berlin and Hamburg indie/electronic scenes underserved
  by major labels — Believe's artist-first positioning resonates
- Key concern: Believe is reorganising German operations — stability
  before growth
- Groove Attack label (Cologne) serves German metal and rock market

### UK — Scale Publishing and Premium
- Context: Revenue growth positively uplifted by Sentric integration
- Strategy: leverage Sentric's UK publishing relationships to cross-sell
  Premium Solutions distribution and marketing services
- Opportunity: UK has Europe's strongest independent music market
  (52% of chart acts are independent)
- Key metric to track: Premium Solutions upsells from Sentric client base

### France — Defend and Expand
- Context: Revenue up 10.3% in FY 2024 — core domestic market
- Strategy: maintain leadership in French rap and electronic, expand
  into adjacent genres via Avant-Garde label (rap, Afro, R&B)
- Opportunity: French streaming market has domestic preference —
  61% of top charts are French-language music
- Key relationship: Deezer (French company) — Believe has home-market
  advantage in editorial relationships

## Artist Development Philosophy

Believe's strategic differentiation is the upsell pipeline from
Automated Solutions (TuneCore) to Premium Solutions (Believe Artist
Services). This is explicitly tracked in results — 1 in 5 TuneCore
Accelerator artists advanced to a higher segment in 2024, with
median royalty earnings increasing fivefold.

Key strategic implication for the market intelligence report: any
trend that increases the number of viable independent artists in
DE/UK/FR markets is an opportunity for Believe's upsell pipeline.
Any trend that erodes independent artist revenues (platform policy
changes, streaming fraud, AI-generated content dilution) is a threat
to the top of that funnel.

## Long-Term Financial Targets

- Long-term Adjusted EBITDA margin target: at least 15%
- FY 2021-2025 revenue CAGR target: 22–25% (set at IPO)
- Segment Adjusted EBITDA margin (high growth period): 15–16%

The privatisation in 2024 is expected to accelerate investment
decisions and reduce short-term earnings pressure, allowing more
aggressive positioning in strategic markets.
