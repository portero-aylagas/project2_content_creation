# Independent Music Industry

Global recorded music revenues reached $36.2 billion in 2024 according to MIDiA Research, or $29.6 billion according to the IFPI Global Music Report — the difference reflects methodology. YoY growth was 6.5% (MIDiA) or 4.8% (IFPI) with growth rate slowing versus 2023. Streaming revenue was $22.2 billion representing 61.3% of all revenues. Streaming growth slowed to 6.2% in 2024 versus 10.3% in 2023. Vinyl sales grew 4.6% in 2024 — the 18th consecutive year of growth. Non-major label (independent) market share was 29.7% in 2024 on a revenue basis, up from 29.2% in 2023. On an ownership basis the independent share is 46.7% globally (MIDiA 2024) — this higher figure includes self-releasing artists retaining ownership of their masters. Non-major label revenues were $10.7 billion in 2024, up 8.2% YoY, growing faster than major labels for the third consecutive year. Artists Direct (self-releasing) revenues were $2.0 billion in 2024, up 4.7% YoY — growth slowing due to minimum stream thresholds. MIDiA Research stated in March 2025: "The much-anticipated streaming revenue deceleration has now arrived." Industry attention is turning to super-premium streaming tiers, superfan monetisation, and expanded rights including sync, live, and merchandise. Independent music is growing for five structural reasons. First, ownership: independent artists retain masters which are increasingly valuable as streaming royalties compound over time. Second, speed: independent releases go live in 24–48 hours versus 6–12 month major label cycles. Third, data access: artists now have direct streaming analytics previously gatekept by labels. Fourth, fan relationships: direct-to-fan monetisation via Bandcamp, Patreon, and Substack creates revenue streams outside streaming. Fifth, AI production tools: AI mixing and mastering reduces production costs, democratising access to professional-quality sound. Over 100,000 new songs are uploaded to streaming services every day as of 2024–2025, up from approximately 60,000 two years prior. This intense competition for editorial attention is the core problem Believe's Premium Solutions solves. Patreon reports an average $52 yearly spend per fan and $110 per paying member — significantly higher than per-stream royalties. 67% more musicians are earning from subscriptions than five years ago. Virtual shows are reducing touring costs: some mid-level bands report traditional tour expenses exceeding £34,000 in 2024. The major labels are systematically acquiring the infrastructure that independent artists depend on — their strategy is acquisition not innovation. Key acquisitions: UMG acquired CD Baby (via Downtown Music Holdings $775M, February 2026), FUGA (same deal), and Songtrust (same deal), as well as INgrooves (earlier). Sony owns AWAL (acquired from Kobalt $430M, 2021) and The Orchard (acquired fully 2015). Believe's genuine independence becomes a stronger differentiator each time a previously "independent" platform is acquired by a major. This consolidation trend is the most important structural dynamic for Believe to monitor in monthly reporting. AI's impact on independent music: 60 million people used AI to create music in 2024 — 10% of consumers (International Music Summit Business Report). AI tools reduce production costs dramatically and democratise access to professional-grade production. Spotify removed 75 million fake and spam tracks in 2024. Deezer receives 30,000+ AI-generated tracks per day with approximately 70% of total Deezer streams linked to artificial plays. Platforms are implementing minimum stream thresholds specifically to address AI-generated streaming fraud. Regulatory and legal developments: UMG, ABKCO, and Concord filed a $500M+ lawsuit against Believe and TuneCore in November 2024 including AI and copyright infringement allegations. Industry debate is ongoing about whether AI-generated music should be eligible for royalty payments. Europe-specific data: Europe contributed 33% of Believe's total FY 2024 revenues. Europe dominates global music publishing collections at €6.445 billion in 2024, up 7.3% YoY, representing more than half of world's total music publishing revenues. Independent publishers generated €2.7 billion globally in 2024. Germany and UK are Europe's top two vinyl markets by units sold. European live music revenue exceeded pre-pandemic levels in 2024 for the first time. Germany and UK have the strongest independent live circuits in Europe across Berlin, Hamburg, Cologne, Munich (Germany) and London, Manchester, Bristol, Glasgow (UK). Key European music festivals for independent artists: Rock en Seine and Solidays in France, Glastonbury BBC Introducing stage in UK, Reeperbahn Festival in Germany.
