## 2026 January

### Market Size
IFPI preliminary data confirmed global recorded music revenues grew 5.9% in 2025 to approximately $38.4 billion — down from 6.5% growth in 2024, confirming deceleration trend. Streaming revenue approximately $23.6 billion in 2025, representing 61.5% of total revenues. Streaming growth slowed to 5.4% in 2025 vs 6.2% in 2024. Non-major (independent) label revenue share reached 30.1% in 2025 — first time crossing 30% on a revenue basis. Independent music ownership-basis share (including self-releasing artists retaining masters) reached 47.2% globally, up from 46.7% in 2024. Sources: IFPI https://www.ifpi.org/resources/ — MIDiA Research https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog

### Artist Economy
Patreon reported 340,000 musicians earning from subscriptions in January 2026, up 8% YoY. Average annual fan spend on Patreon creator subscriptions reached $58, up from $52 in 2025. Bandcamp reported independent music sales of $72 million in Q4 2025, with 82% going directly to artists. Artists Direct (self-releasing) revenues reached $2.1 billion in 2025, up 5.0% YoY. Over 100,000 new songs uploaded to streaming services daily. European live music market generated €3.2 billion in Q4 2025 — above pre-pandemic equivalent for second consecutive year. UK, Germany, and France accounted for 61% of European live music revenue in Q4 2025. Sources: Patreon creator economy report — Bandcamp https://daily.bandcamp.com — IFPI https://www.ifpi.org/resources/

### Industry Consolidation
Major labels are systematically acquiring independent distribution infrastructure. Key acquisitions: UMG acquired CD Baby, FUGA, and Songtrust via $775M Downtown Music Holdings purchase (February 2026); Sony owns AWAL ($430M, 2021) and The Orchard (2015). MIDiA estimated 34,000 CD Baby artists actively evaluating alternative distributors following the acquisition. Believe's genuine independence becomes a stronger differentiator each time a previously "independent" platform is acquired by a major. Sources: Alera FM https://www.alera.fm/blog/who-owns-your-music-distributor-2026 — MIDiA Research https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog

## 2026 February

### Market Size
MIDiA Research February 2026 confirmed independent music revenue grew 8.6% in 2025 vs 5.1% growth for major label music — third consecutive year of independent outperformance. Independent sector's 30.1% revenue share represents a structural shift driven by artist ownership preferences, improved distribution infrastructure, and platform accessibility. Sources: MIDiA Research https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog

### Artist Economy
AI-generated music detection across platforms flagged 34 million tracks in January–February 2026 combined — Spotify removed 38 million in full-year 2024, indicating flagging velocity has increased significantly. Independent artist revenue from sync licensing grew 22% in 2025, driven by YouTube Creator Music partnerships and TikTok Commercial Music Library. European music publishing collections reached €6.8 billion in 2025, up from €6.445 billion in 2024. Independent publishers generated €2.9 billion globally in 2025 — up 7.4% YoY. Sources: Water & Music https://www.waterandmusic.com — IMPF https://www.impforum.org

### Industry Consolidation
Independent Artist Coalition issued statement in February encouraging members to consider UMG's ownership of CD Baby when making distribution decisions. Artist manager community response to CD Baby's Heritage Artist Programme was largely negative, citing UMG ownership concerns. 🟢 Opportunity for Believe: artist migration from major-owned "independent-branded" platforms is accelerating. Sources: Independent Artist Coalition https://www.independentartistcoalition.com — Hypebot https://www.hypebot.com

## 2026 March

### Market Size
IFPI Q1 2026 preliminary confirmed global streaming revenues grew 5.7% YoY in Q1, with Europe leading at 6.3% growth. IFPI attributed European outperformance to stronger independent music growth in Germany, UK, and France — all three markets showing independent act streaming growth ahead of their respective market averages. Independent music ownership-basis share tracking toward 47.8% for full-year 2026 based on Q1 trajectory. Vinyl sales grew 7.1% YoY in Q1 2026 — 20th consecutive year of vinyl growth. Sources: IFPI https://www.ifpi.org/resources/

### Artist Economy
Superfan monetisation platforms reported combined Q1 2026 revenue of $820 million, up 28% YoY — on track to exceed $3 billion for full-year 2026. Patreon's music creator tier grew by 18,000 artists in Q1 2026. TikTok-to-Spotify conversion rate for independent artists improved to 4.2% in March from 3.1% in March 2025 — reflecting improved artist understanding of cross-platform fan funnel optimisation. Sources: MIDiA Research https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog

### Industry Consolidation
Independent Artist Coalition launched "Distribution Transparency" campaign in March following the CD Baby acquisition, creating a public database of distributor ownership structures. Campaign reached 2.1 million social media impressions in its first week — high artist awareness of ownership consolidation issue. TuneCore and Believe identified as the most-recommended genuinely independent options in the database. Sources: Independent Artist Coalition https://www.independentartistcoalition.com

## 2026 April

### Market Size
MIDiA Research April 2026 confirmed independent music revenue share reached 30.4% in Q1 2026 — up from 30.1% in Q4 2025 — indicating the 30% milestone is stable rather than a one-time crossing. Gap between major label growth rates (approximately 4.5% in 2025) and independent growth rates (approximately 8.6% in 2025) is widening — suggesting structural market share shift rather than cyclical variation. MIDiA highlighted Believe's FY 2024 revenue of €988.8 million as evidence that genuine independent distributors are scaling effectively. Sources: MIDiA Research https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog

### Artist Economy
Global vinyl sales Q1 2026 grew 7.1% YoY — 20th consecutive year of growth. Superfan platform revenue crossed $1 billion annualised run rate in April for the first time — Patreon, Bandcamp, and direct artist subscription platforms combined. AI music detection tools across Spotify, Deezer, and Apple Music collectively removed 18 million AI-generated tracks in Q1 2026. Independent artist sync licensing revenue from YouTube Creator Music partnerships projected to grow 30% in 2026. Sources: MIDiA Research https://www.midiaresearch.com/blog — IFPI https://www.ifpi.org/resources/ — Water & Music https://www.waterandmusic.com

### Industry Consolidation
Distribution Transparency database reached 45,000 artist queries in April — TuneCore and Believe most recommended genuinely independent options. Independent Artist Coalition campaign social reach grew to 3.8 million impressions by end of April. The CD Baby to TuneCore migration is the most significant ongoing redistribution event in the independent music market since the AWAL acquisition by Sony in 2021. Structural implication for Believe: each major label acquisition of an "independent" platform strengthens Believe's genuine independence narrative and grows its addressable market of independence-seeking artists. Sources: Independent Artist Coalition https://www.independentartistcoalition.com — Alera FM https://www.alera.fm/blog/who-owns-your-music-distributor-2026
