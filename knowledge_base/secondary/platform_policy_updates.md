1,000-stream minimum threshold confirmed unchanged for 2026 — no retroactive adjustments. Emerging Artist badge launched for acts with fewer than 50,000 monthly listeners, visible to editorial curators in the pitch portal. Pitch portal processed 2.3 million pitches in January — new monthly record. Impact on Believe: 🔴 Risk (threshold unchanged) — TuneCore early-stage artists below threshold continue to receive zero royalties. 🟢 Opportunity (badge) — ensure all qualifying roster acts have activated the badge so curators can discover them. ## Apple Music
Published first public editorial pitching guidelines — clarifies one-shot-per-release pitch rule, confirms 14-day minimum pitch lead time, lists available mood and genre tags. New playlist performance analytics dashboard launched showing add and drop rates, listener retention per playlist, and cross-playlist listener overlap. Dolby Atmos royalty bonus (approximately 10% uplift) remains in effect — Atmos streams now 70% of all Apple Music plays. Impact on Believe: 🟢 Opportunity — update Artist Solutions pitching workflow to align with 14-day minimum; ensure all acts have Dolby Atmos masters to capture full royalty uplift. Sources: Apple Music for Artists https://artists.apple.com — Chartlex https://www.chartlex.com/blog/streaming/apple-music-for-artists-complete-guide-2026

## Deezer
Artist-centric (user-centric) payment model expanded to France and Germany — each subscriber's fee distributed only among artists they actually listened to rather than pro-rata pooling. Believe artists in these markets saw average 12% royalty increase in first month. AI detection system removed 4.1 million AI-generated tracks in January — one of the first platforms to act decisively on AI content. Impact on Believe: 🟢 Opportunity — artist-centric model benefits Believe's genuine artists with loyal fanbases; communicate the royalty benefit to France and Germany roster as a Premium Solutions value-add. Sources: Deezer newsroom https://www.deezer.com/en/company/news

## YouTube Music
Launched "New Independent" weekly editorial playlist in Germany and France featuring 30 independent acts — Believe has editorial submission access via YouTube Music partnership. Impact on Believe: 🟢 Opportunity — submit candidates weekly in Germany and France to establish playlist presence. Sources: YouTube Music for Artists https://artists.youtube.com
