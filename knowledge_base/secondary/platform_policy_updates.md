# Platform Policy Updates

This file is updated monthly with the latest policy changes, feature launches, and strategic moves from major streaming platforms. Each entry follows the format: platform name, change name, date, what changed, impact on artists, and relevance to Believe with a green/yellow/red signal.

Spotify — 1,000-stream minimum threshold. Date: April 2024, ongoing. Tracks must reach 1,000 streams per year to qualify for royalty payments, effectively demonetising approximately 60% of tracks on the platform. Revenue is redistributed to higher-stream tracks. Developed in partnership with major labels including Universal Music Group. Impact: significant for early-stage self-releasing artists who may have small but genuine fanbases. Relevance to Believe: red risk for TuneCore tier — reduces viability of early-stage artists and may increase churn. Also creates urgency for TuneCore Accelerator to help artists cross the threshold, which is an opportunity for upsell to Premium Solutions. Source: https://www.unchainedmusic.io/blog-posts/spotifys-new-royalty-policy-may-leave-many-artists-unpaid

Spotify — music videos official launch. Date: 2025. Music videos exited beta and rolled out to 100+ global markets. Artists can attach official videos and live performances to tracks. Listeners can switch between audio and video within the app. Impact: new content type to create and optimise. Drives higher engagement and longer session times. Relevance to Believe: green opportunity — Believe's video distribution capabilities (already part of Premium Solutions) become more strategically valuable. Source: https://routenote.com/blog/spotify-for-artists-2025-a-recap-on-all-the-new-features/

Spotify — AI content policy update. Date: 2025, ongoing. Updated policy to protect artists from AI impersonation, require AI disclosure labels, and implement automated fraud detection. Removed 75 million spam and fake tracks in 2024. Cracking down on vocal cloning. Impact: genuine artists benefit from a cleaner platform with less competition from AI-generated content for editorial placements. Artists must now disclose AI usage in releases. Relevance to Believe: green opportunity — cleanup improves editorial signal quality and Believe's genuine artists benefit as fraudulent streams are removed from the royalty pool. Source: https://imusician.pro/en/resources/blog/spotify-ai-policy-in-2025-artist-protection-transparency

Spotify — ChatGPT integration. Date: October 2025. Spotify integrated into ChatGPT in 145 countries. Users receive personalised music recommendations via conversational AI using emotional and descriptive prompts such as "calm indie songs for late-night focus". Impact: new discovery pathway via natural language description. Mood-based and niche artists may benefit more than mainstream pop acts. Relevance to Believe: green opportunity — Believe's indie and niche roster in DE, UK, and FR are well-positioned for discovery via descriptive prompts. Source: https://imusician.pro/en/resources/blog/how-spotify-changes-affect-the-music-industry

Spotify — pre-save hub launch. Date: late 2024. Dedicated hub showing top 10 most pre-saved upcoming releases updated daily. Artists and labels can track pre-save counts as a campaign metric. High pre-save counts create social proof and are now publicly visible. Relevance to Believe: green opportunity — Believe Premium Solutions team can use pre-save count as a KPI for release campaigns and demonstrate ROI to artists more clearly. Source: https://rebelmusicdistribution.com/2025/09/30/spotify-removes-75-million-fake-tracks-in-2024-what-it-means-for-artists-in-2025/

Apple Music — Dolby Atmos royalty bonus confirmed ongoing. Date: January 2024 confirmed, still in effect 2025–2026. Apple Music pays an approximately 10% royalty bonus on streams played in Dolby Atmos. Atmos listening is now estimated at 65–75% of all Apple Music plays in 2026. Artists without Atmos masters are leaving 6–8% of their Apple Music revenue uncollected. Over 12 million tracks now mastered in Dolby Atmos. Relevance to Believe: green opportunity — Believe can add Atmos mastering guidance to Premium Solutions artist services; TuneCore Mastering tool launched Q2 2024 could be upgraded. Source: https://www.chartlex.com/blog/streaming/apple-music-for-artists-complete-guide-2026

Apple Music — per-stream payout 2025–2026. Current rate $0.008–$0.010 per stream, approximately double Spotify's rate. Mechanism: no free tier means every stream is from a paying subscriber. A release earning 100,000 streams split 70/30 Spotify/Apple Music earns more total revenue from the 30,000 Apple Music streams than from the 70,000 Spotify streams. Relevance to Believe: green opportunity — for Believe's artists in markets where Apple Music has 18–20% share (UK, Germany), optimising for Apple Music editorial is high ROI. Source: https://www.chartlex.com/blog/streaming/apple-music-for-artists-complete-guide-2026

Deezer — AI content detection and exclusion. Date: 2024–2025, ongoing. Detection tools tag AI-generated content, exclude AI tracks from recommendation algorithms, and limit their participation in editorial playlists. Deezer reported receiving 30,000+ AI-generated tracks per day and approximately 70% of total streams linked to artificial plays. One of the first major platforms to act decisively on AI content. Relevance to Believe: green opportunity — Believe's genuine artists benefit from cleaner recommendations. Believe's Deezer editorial relationship is an advantage for French market placement. Source: https://imusician.pro/en/resources/blog/spotify-ai-policy-in-2025-artist-protection-transparency

Deezer — artist-centric payment model experiment. Date: ongoing 2024–2025. Experimenting with distributing each subscriber's fee only to artists that subscriber actually listened to rather than pro-rata pooling. This benefits artists with genuine loyal fanbases and penalises those with inflated passive stream counts. Relevance to Believe: green opportunity — Believe's roster of genuine independent artists benefits under user-centric model. Believe should monitor roll-out and consider advocating for wider adoption. Source: https://www.prolificdaily.com/2025/08/highest-paying-music-streaming-platforms-2025.html

TikTok — Artist Impact Programme active. Date: May 2023 onwards. Believe is a global distribution partner. Believe artists can participate in TikTok's Commercial Music Library and add trending songs. Relevance to Believe: green opportunity — Believe artists have preferential access to TikTok's commercial music ecosystem. Yellow monitor — TikTok's ongoing regulatory issues in various markets create platform risk for artists dependent on it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Believe_Music
