# Market Trends Germany UK France

Germany is the 3rd largest music market in Europe by streaming revenue. Streaming accounts for 83% of music consumption. The market has distinctive characteristics: German-language Deutschpop growing +28% YoY streams, Europe's number one vinyl market by units sold, and a powerful independent scene centred on Berlin, Hamburg, Cologne, and Munich. Streaming platform shares: Spotify approximately 68%, Apple Music 18% strongest with premium listeners, Deezer approximately 8% declining but retaining 25–40 age demographic, YouTube Music growing particularly among 18–24. Believe Germany revenue was €111.3 million in FY 2024, up 0.4% YoY with digital trajectory returning positive after the physical exit strategy. Genre trends: indie pop and indie electronic are growing strongly with Berlin and Hamburg scenes driving European circuit bookings; German-language hip-hop and rap dominate domestic streaming charts; Deutschpop up 28% YoY; electronic and techno with Berlin remaining the global techno capital with strong streaming and live crossover for independent producers; post-punk influenced indie growing following the broader European trend; German indie artists with bilingual German and English output outperform purely English acts in domestic editorial playlists. Media landscape: Musikexpress is the most influential indie print and digital publication; tip Berlin is essential for Berlin-based artists; ByteFM is the influential independent radio for indie and electronic; WDR Cosmo is public radio with indie programming; BVMI (Bundesverband Musikindustrie) publishes quarterly streaming data. Key opportunity: Believe's local presence is an advantage as independent electronic and techno artists from Berlin grow on Spotify DE editorial playlists.

The UK is the 2nd largest music market in Europe. Streaming accounts for 91% of music consumption. 52% of chart acts are independent — Europe's strongest independent music culture. The TikTok-to-streaming discovery pipeline is more developed in the UK than any other European market. Streaming platform shares: Spotify approximately 72%, Apple Music 20% crucial for industry credibility and press coverage, Amazon Music 6% and growing with Prime subscribers, YouTube significant for music video consumption and independent artist discovery. Genre trends: post-punk revival continuing strongly following the Wet Leg and Yard Act effect with labels actively seeking guitar bands; bedroom pop and lo-fi indie growing at +34% YoY streams on Spotify UK indie playlists; hard techno growing fast with Azyr (Blackpool) selling out a 5,000-capacity Manchester venue in under 24 hours after a Boiler Room stream garnered over 2.5 million YouTube views; indie folk has a strong press coverage pipeline via BBC Introducing, MOJO, and Uncut; Afrobeats and UK garage crossover growing rapidly among 16–24. Media landscape: NME is online-first with high traffic covering emerging artists; DIY Magazine is the most respected indie tastemaker; The Line of Best Fit is strong online for emerging artists; MOJO and Uncut target older demographic with album focus; BBC Introducing is the most important platform for emerging UK artists with a direct pipeline to Radio 1 and BBC 6 Music. Key opportunity: Sentric (UK publishing) integration creates cross-sell opportunities as Sentric clients are natural Premium Solutions candidates.

France is the 4th largest music market in Europe. French-language music accounts for 61% of top streaming charts — the highest domestic preference ratio in Europe. Radio remains unusually powerful compared to other European markets. Physical music (vinyl and CD) still accounts for 12% of revenue, the highest in Europe alongside Germany. Streaming platform shares: Spotify 58% lower than UK and Germany due to Deezer's home market advantage; Deezer 28% significantly higher than any other European market as Believe has a home-market advantage via direct Deezer editorial relationships; Apple Music approximately 10%; YouTube very strong for discovery especially 16–24. Genre trends: French rap is the dominant genre with Believe holding a strong roster (KeBlack, Heuss l'Enfoiré, Uzi, Carbonne all charted top positions H1 2024); indie électronique and synthpop have strong scenes in Lyon, Paris, and Bordeaux with international crossover potential via La Blogothèque; indie française artists embracing French cultural identity outperform those mimicking Anglo-American trends; Afropop and Caribbean crossover growing in the Paris metropolitan market. Media landscape: Les Inrockuptibles is the most prestigious indie publication; Télérama is a cultural institution with broad audience; La Blogothèque is internationally read and session videos have launched French artists globally; Radio Nova is influential independent radio accessible to indie artists; FIP (Radio France) is curated and quality-focused playing both international indie and French music; France Inter is mainstream public radio transformative if achieved; FNAC digital and physical retail newsletter reaches 180,000+ subscribers. Key opportunity: Deezer's home market position is Believe's editorial relationship advantage, and French artists distributed by Believe have preferential access compared to competitors.
