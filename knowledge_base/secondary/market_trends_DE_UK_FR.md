# 2026 January

## Germany
Streaming revenue grew 9.1% YoY in Q4 2025, driven by domestic indie pop and German-language hip-hop outperforming international releases on Spotify DE editorial playlists. Spotify holds 68% market share, Apple Music 18%, Deezer 8% declining, YouTube Music growing among 18–24. Berlin independent electronic scene produced 3 Spotify DE editorial placements in January. Musikexpress reported 34% increase in reader engagement with independent artist features versus major label acts. Vinyl sales grew 6.2% in 2025 — Europe's largest vinyl market for the fourth consecutive year. Groove Attack (Cologne) completed physical distribution exit in Q4 2025, digital-only transition effective January 2026. Key opportunity: Spotify DE editorial appetite for bilingual German-English indie acts remains strong. Sources: BVMI Q4 2025 https://www.musikindustrie.de/en — Believe FY 2025 preliminary https://www.believe.com/investors

## UK
Streaming accounted for 93% of music consumption in January 2026, up from 91% in 2024. Spotify holds 72% market share, Apple Music 21%, Amazon Music 6%. BBC Introducing received 12,400 submissions in January — 19% increase YoY, highest volume on record. Post-punk and guitar-led indie acts dominated New Music Friday UK with 6 of 10 new additions being independent. Azyr (Blackpool) announced second sold-out show at Manchester Factory International. The Line of Best Fit reported independent artists accounted for 58% of January coverage, up from 52% in 2025. Key opportunity: BBC Introducing North, Midlands, Wales slots underutilised by Believe's current UK roster. Sources: BPI January 2026 https://www.bpi.co.uk/industry-insights

## France
Streaming grew 7.8% YoY in Q4 2025. Deezer holds 28% market share — highest domestic share in Europe. Spotify 59%, Apple Music 10%. French-language music accounted for 63% of top streaming charts, up from 61% in 2024. Believe's French rap roster placed 4 tracks in French Spotify top 20. La Blogothèque published 3 session videos in January driving average 280,000 YouTube views each within 7 days. Radio Nova and FIP added combined 11 independent tracks to rotation. Key opportunity: Deezer Nouvelle Scène Française playlist has open slots for February — Believe's editorial relationship gives priority access. Sources: SNEP January 2026 https://www.snepmusique.com/en

## Global
IFPI preliminary data: global recorded music revenues grew 5.9% in 2025, down from 6.5% in 2024, confirming deceleration trend. Streaming growth slowed to 5.4% in 2025 vs 6.2% in 2024. Independent music ownership-basis market share reached 47.2% globally, up from 46.7% in 2024. Vinyl sales grew for 19th consecutive year. Sources: IFPI https://www.ifpi.org/resources/
