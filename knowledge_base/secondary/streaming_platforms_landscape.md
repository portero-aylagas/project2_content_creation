# Streaming Platforms Landscape

Spotify holds approximately 31% of global music streaming market share. Monthly active users exceed 700 million including 281 million paying subscribers across 237 countries. Per-stream payout is $0.003–$0.005, the lowest of major paid platforms. Total royalties paid 2014–2024 exceeded $10 billion. Approximately 12 million artists distribute through the platform. Over 100,000 new songs are uploaded daily as of 2024–2025. Streaming (led by Spotify's 31% share) represented over 69% of total recorded music revenue worldwide by 2025.

Key Spotify policy changes 2024–2025: (1) 1,000-stream minimum threshold introduced April 2024 — tracks must reach 1,000 streams per year to qualify for royalty payments, effectively demonetising approximately 60% of tracks on the platform with revenue redistributed to higher-stream tracks. This was developed in partnership with major labels. Impact on Believe: red risk for TuneCore tier — reduces viability of early-stage self-releasing artists. (2) AI content policy 2025 — Spotify updated policy to require AI disclosure labels, protect artists from impersonation, and implement automated fraud detection. Removed 75 million spam and fake tracks in 2024. Impact on Believe: green opportunity — cleanup improves editorial playlist signal quality benefiting genuine independent artists. (3) Music videos officially launched 2025 — rolled out to 100+ global markets, fans can switch between audio and video in-app. Impact on Believe: green opportunity — adds a content type Believe can help artists create and optimise for. (4) ChatGPT integration October 2025 — Spotify integrated into ChatGPT in 145 countries allowing personalised music recommendations via conversational AI based on emotional and descriptive prompts. Impact on Believe: green opportunity — niche and mood-based independent artists may benefit from discovery via natural language. (5) Pre-save hub launched late 2024 — dedicated hub showing top 10 most pre-saved upcoming releases updated daily, artists and labels can track pre-save counts. Impact on Believe: green opportunity — Believe can use this as a release campaign KPI.

Key Spotify editorial playlists for Believe's markets: Germany — Neue Musik Freitag 890k followers, Deutsch Pop 1.2M followers, Berlin Sounds 210k followers; UK — New Music Friday UK 1.8M followers, Indie List 950k followers, Hot New Bands 450k followers; France — Nouvelle Scène Française 780k followers, Indie France 420k followers, Les Incontournables 1.1M followers. Spotify editorial pitch deadline is minimum 7 days before release, recommended 28 days. Apple Music holds an estimated 12–15% of global paid streaming subscribers, approximately 95–110 million paying users worldwide as of 2026. Per-stream payout is $0.008–$0.010 — approximately double Spotify's rate. Every stream is from a paying subscriber as there is no meaningful free tier, which is why the per-stream rate is higher. Apple pays an additional approximately 10% royalty bonus on streams played in Dolby Atmos. Atmos listening crossed 50% of Apple Music streams in late 2024 and is estimated at 65–75% of Apple Music plays in 2026, meaning artists without Atmos masters are leaving 6–8% of their Apple Music revenue uncollected. Over 12 million tracks are now mastered in Dolby Atmos. The Dolby Atmos royalty bonus policy was confirmed in January 2024 and remains in effect. Pitching is a one-shot submission per release via the Apple Music for Artists app. A release earning 100,000 streams split 70/30 between Spotify and Apple Music earns more total revenue from the 30,000 Apple Music streams than from the 70,000 Spotify streams. Impact on Believe: green opportunity — Believe can add Atmos mastering guidance to Premium Solutions artist services; TuneCore Mastering tool launched Q2 2024 could be upgraded to include Atmos optimisation. Deezer holds approximately 6–8% global market share but 28% in France — dramatically higher than the global average due to its French home market identity. It is experimenting with an artist-centric (user-centric) payment model where each subscriber's fee is distributed only among artists that subscriber actually listened to, rather than pooling all revenue by total platform streams. This model benefits niche and indie artists with loyal fanbases. Deezer receives over 30,000 AI-generated tracks per day and reports approximately 70% of total streams are linked to artificial plays. It deployed detection tools to tag AI-generated content, excludes AI tracks from recommendation algorithms, and limits their participation in editorial playlists — one of the first major platforms to act decisively on AI content. Impact on Believe: green opportunity — Deezer's French home market position is one of Believe's key editorial relationship advantages that competitors lack. The artist-centric model benefits Believe's artists with genuine fanbases. TikTok drives 60% of Spotify streams for emerging artists according to MusicWatch 2024. It is the dominant discovery mechanism for under-25 demographic across DE, UK, and FR. The TikTok-to-streaming pipeline is most developed in the UK among European markets. Believe is a global partner in the TikTok Artist Impact Programme since May 2023, allowing Believe artists to participate in TikTok's Commercial Music Library. Impact on Believe: green opportunity — Believe artists have preferential access to TikTok's commercial music ecosystem. Yellow monitor — TikTok's ongoing regulatory issues in various markets create platform risk for artists dependent on it. YouTube: Believe has been a YouTube Creator Music partner since September 2022, enabling sync revenue for Believe artists via YouTube creator licensing deals. YouTube Music is growing particularly among 18–24 in Germany.

Platform payout rates comparison 2025–2026: Tidal $0.0125 per stream with approximately 3 million subscribers and artist-owned structure; Apple Music $0.008–$0.010 with 95–110 million subscribers and Dolby Atmos bonus; Amazon Music $0.004–$0.006 with approximately 100 million subscribers driven by Prime bundle; Spotify $0.003–$0.005 with 281 million subscribers and best discovery tools; Deezer $0.003–$0.005 with approximately 10 million subscribers experimenting with artist-centric model; YouTube Music $0.001–$0.002 with growing subscribers and combined video and audio.
