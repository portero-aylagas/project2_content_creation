Per-stream payout $0.003–$0.005. 1,000-stream minimum threshold confirmed unchanged for 2026. New "Emerging Artist" badge launched for acts with fewer than 50,000 monthly listeners — visible to editorial curators in pitch portal. Spotify for Artists pitch portal processed 2.3 million pitches in January — new monthly record. Impact on Believe: 🟢 Opportunity — ensure all qualifying roster acts have activated the Emerging Artist badge so curators can discover them. Dolby Atmos streams crossed 70% of all Apple Music plays in January — up from 65% in Q3 2025. Published first public editorial guidelines clarifying the one-shot-per-release pitch rule and confirming 14-day minimum pitch lead time. New playlist performance analytics dashboard added showing playlist add and drop rates. Per-stream payout remains $0.008–$0.010. Impact on Believe: 🟢 Opportunity — published guidelines remove ambiguity; update Artist Solutions pitching workflow to align with 14-day minimum. Impact on Believe: 🟢 Opportunity — artist-centric model expansion in France and Germany directly benefits Believe's roster of genuine independent artists with loyal fanbases.
