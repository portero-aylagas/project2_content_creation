### Corporate Strategy
Four focus areas for FY 2025–2026: value optimisation (exit low-margin physical distribution, focus on digital-first high-margin services); controlled investment and selective acquisitions (bolt-on M&A following Sentric, PlayCode, Krumulo model); automation and efficiency (Be Odyssey programme, TuneCore Accelerator at scale for 462,000+ artists); market share growth (+13% organic revenue target assumes streaming share gains across Europe and Americas). Long-term EBITDA margin target: at least 15%.

### Germany
Stabilise and digitalise. Groove Attack physical distribution exit completed January 2026 — fully digital-only operations. Three Berlin electronic acts shortlisted for Premium Solutions onboarding in Q1 2026. Independent electronic and techno scene underserved by major labels — Believe's artist-first positioning resonates. Spotify DE editorial appetite for bilingual German-English indie acts consistently strong. Key priority: accelerate Premium Solutions outreach to high-potential German independent acts before AWAL's expanded Berlin A&R team makes contact.

### UK
Scale publishing and Premium Solutions via Sentric cross-sell. Sentric pipeline generated 34 new Premium Solutions conversations in January 2026 — conversion rate 18%, up from 12% in Q4 2025. 52% of UK chart acts are independent — large addressable market for Premium Solutions tier. BBC Introducing is the most important platform for emerging UK artists; Believe's local team supports pitching strategy.

### France
Defend home market, expand into adjacent genres via Avant-Garde label (rap, Afro, R&B). Avant-Garde signed 2 new Afropop acts in January 2026. Deezer home market editorial relationship is a structural advantage unavailable to competitors. French-language music accounts for 63% of top streaming charts — strong domestic preference benefits Believe's French roster. Key risk: Virgin Music opened Paris office January 2026 and is actively signing French independent acts.

### Upsell Pipeline
TuneCore to Premium Solutions upsell is Believe's most important strategic differentiator. TuneCore Accelerator enrolled 462,000 artists as of January 2026. Conversion milestones: 890 in January, 940 in February, 1,020 in April (first month above 1,000). Advancement rate improved from 1-in-5 (2024 baseline) to 1-in-4.7 in February 2026. Any trend increasing the number of viable independent artists in DE, UK, and FR is an opportunity for the upsell funnel. Any trend eroding independent artist revenues — platform stream thresholds, AI content dilution — is a threat to the top of that funnel.
