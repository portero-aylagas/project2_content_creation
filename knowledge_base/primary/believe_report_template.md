# Believe Report Template

This file defines the structure, tone, and content requirements for Believe's monthly market intelligence report. The AI system reads this file to understand how to format and frame every report it generates.

Report identity: name is Believe Market Intelligence Report, frequency is monthly, audience is Believe internal strategy team and Artist Solutions managers, markets covered are Germany, UK, and France as primary with global highlights, tone is professional, data-driven, and concise with no filler — every sentence must contain a fact, an insight, or an action recommendation, target length is 800–1,200 words readable in 5 minutes.

The report must follow this exact section order every month. Section 1 is the executive summary: 3–4 sentences maximum in plain prose with no bullets. It states the 3 most important things that happened this month across markets, competition, and platforms. It answers what this means for Believe specifically and what one action Believe should consider. Section 2 is market trends: 200–250 words, one paragraph per market (Germany, UK, France) followed by a global highlights paragraph. Each market paragraph must include at least 2 specific statistics, dominant platform shifts, genre trends, and notable chart movements. Section 3 is platform updates: 150–200 words in bullet format, one bullet per platform update (Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, YouTube Music, TikTok music-related only), 2–3 sentences each. Each bullet must carry a flag: green for opportunity benefiting independent artists, yellow for monitor, red for risk negatively impacting independent artist revenue or visibility. Section 4 is competitor intelligence: 200–250 words, one paragraph per competitor (DistroKid, AWAL / The Orchard, Virgin Music Group, TuneCore internal watch). Content per competitor covers pricing changes, new product launches, new market entries, notable artist signings, marketing shifts, and funding or acquisition news. Each competitor entry must close with a threat rating: red for high threat requiring immediate strategic response, yellow for monitor 30 days, green for noted no action required. Section 5 is the independent artist economy: 150–200 words in plain prose with key statistics bolded, covering market share movement, artist earnings data, emerging revenue models, distribution volume trends, and artist churn between platforms. Section 6 is market opportunities: 150–200 words presenting exactly 3 numbered opportunities, each with a bold opportunity name on one line, 1–2 sentences of evidence with data, and 1 sentence of recommended Believe action. Opportunities must be specific to Believe's business, not generic industry observations. Section 7 is data sources used: a bullet list of every source referenced in the report with name, URL, and date accessed.

Formatting rules that apply to every section: always lead with data, never open with a general observation; name names — reference specific platforms, artists, companies, and markets, never say "a major platform" when you mean Spotify; every trend needs a number, "growing" is not enough, "growing 8.2% YoY" is acceptable; one insight per paragraph; at least one actionable recommendation per section; no filler phrases including "it is worth noting", "it is interesting that", "as we can see"; consistent market order is always Germany then UK then France; flag every competitor move with the red, yellow, green system in Section 4.

The report header to use every month: BELIEVE MARKET INTELLIGENCE REPORT / Month: [MONTH YEAR] / Generated: [DATE] / Markets: Germany | UK | France / Sources: [NUMBER] data points reviewed / followed by a divider line.

Quality checklist before finalising: executive summary is under 4 sentences; every market section has at least 2 statistics; every platform update has a flag; every competitor section has a threat rating; Section 6 has exactly 3 opportunities with recommended actions; all sources are listed in Section 7; total word count is between 800 and 1,200 words; no section uses the word "interesting" or "notable".
