# Believe Company Profile

Believe is a French digital music company founded in Paris in 2005 by Denis Ladegaillerie. It is the world's largest independent digital music distributor, operating across more than 50 countries with approximately 2,020 employees. The company distributes music to over 200 streaming platforms and digital stores worldwide. Believe is not owned by a major label — this genuine independence is a core part of its brand identity and competitive positioning. In April 2024, Believe was taken majority-private by a consortium including founder Denis Ladegaillerie and investment firms TCV and EQT, who together held 96.63% of share capital by mid-2024. Prior to privatisation, Believe was listed on Euronext Paris (Ticker: BLV).

Mission: "To develop independent artists and labels in the digital world by providing them the solutions they need to grow their audience at each stage of their career and development." Core operating principles are expertise, respect, fairness, and transparency.

FY 2024 financial results: total revenues €988.8 million ($1.073 billion), up 12.3% YoY. Digital Music Sales €1,311.7 million, up 8.3% YoY. Adjusted EBITDA €67.1 million, up 33.5% vs 2023, margin 6.8%. Net cash position €139.8 million after €91 million in M&A outflows. Artists generated 800+ billion streams globally in 2024. 77+ awards won by Believe artists globally. Revenue split: Premium Solutions €942.2 million (11.2% organic growth), Automated Solutions TuneCore €64.6 million (15.9% organic growth). Geographic revenue: Europe excluding France and Germany up 23.3% YoY representing 33% of total; France up 10.3%; Germany up 0.4% (stable, digital trajectory positive after physical exit); Americas up 18% representing 15.3% of total; Asia/Pacific/Africa up 3.5%. FY 2025 outlook: organic growth above +13.0%, Adjusted EBITDA margin target ~8.0%, EBITDA growth at least +35% vs FY 2024, positive and increasing free cash flow.

Believe operates a dual-platform model. Premium Solutions is full-service distribution and marketing for established and growing artists on a revenue-share basis. It includes editorial playlist pitching, press outreach, paid social campaigns, video distribution, artist development, and publishing administration. A dedicated Artist Solutions manager is assigned per campaign. Target tier is artists with 50,000+ monthly Spotify listeners. Automated Solutions is led by TuneCore, the self-service DIY platform for emerging and self-releasing artists on a flat annual subscription fee model. TuneCore Accelerator is the flagship marketing programme launched December 2023, now enrolling 450,000+ self-releasing artists. 1 in 5 Accelerator artists advanced to a higher segment in 2024, with median royalty earnings increasing fivefold. TuneCore Mastering tool was added Q2 2024.

Key portfolio brands: Believe (core artist services), TuneCore (DIY distribution), Nuclear Blast (metal label), Naïve (French independent label), Groove Attack (German distribution, Cologne), AllPoints (US label services), Sentric (music publishing administration, acquired March 2023), Ishtar, Byond.

Germany is Believe's focus for digital restructuring — revenue grew 0.4% in FY 2024 as Groove Attack exits physical distribution contracts. UK revenue growth was positively uplifted by the Sentric publishing integration. France is the core home market with revenue up 10.3%; strong French rap roster includes KeBlack, Heuss l'Enfoiré, Uzi, Carbonne. Americas grew 18%. New market entries in 2024: PlayCode imprint in Japan (hip-hop), Krumulo imprint in Indonesia (Java Pop), acquisition of Turkish label DMC, 25% stake in Global Records (Romania). White Hill Music Punjabi catalog acquired December 2023.

Technology platform is called the Central Platform. Be Odyssey programme launched 2024 to further optimise platform capabilities. Backstage is Believe's proprietary artist analytics tool covering real-time streaming data, audience demographics, playlist tracking, and competitor benchmarking. Believe is an AI partnership explorer since April 2023, focusing on marketing and promotion applications. Believe joined YouTube Creator Music as an independent partner September 2022 and TikTok Artist Impact Programme as a global partner May 2023.
