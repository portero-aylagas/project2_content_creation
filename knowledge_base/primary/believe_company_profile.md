## 2026 January

### Overview
Believe is a French digital music company founded in Paris in 2005 by Denis Ladegaillerie. World's largest independent digital music distributor, operating across 50+ countries with approximately 2,020 employees, distributing to 200+ platforms. Not owned by a major label — genuine independence is its primary competitive differentiator. Taken majority-private April 2024 by a consortium of Denis Ladegaillerie, TCV, and EQT holding 96.63% of share capital. Mission: develop independent artists and labels in the digital world at each stage of their career. Core principles: expertise, respect, fairness, transparency.

### Business Model
Premium Solutions: full-service distribution and marketing for artists with 50,000+ monthly Spotify listeners on a revenue-share basis. Includes editorial playlist pitching, press outreach, paid social campaigns, video distribution, artist development, publishing administration, dedicated Artist Solutions manager per campaign. Automated Solutions: TuneCore self-service DIY platform for emerging artists on flat annual subscription fee. TuneCore Accelerator marketing programme: 462,000 artists enrolled as of January 2026, 1 in 5 advancing to a higher segment in 2024 with median royalty earnings increasing fivefold. TuneCore Mastering tool launched Q2 2024.

### Financials
FY 2024: total revenues €988.8 million up 12.3% YoY. Adjusted EBITDA €67.1 million up 33.5%. 800+ billion streams by Believe artists globally. 77+ awards. Revenue by geography: Europe excl. France and Germany up 23.3% representing 33% of total; France up 10.3%; Germany up 0.4%; Americas up 18%; Asia/Pacific/Africa up 3.5%. FY 2025 outlook: organic growth above +13.0%, EBITDA margin ~8.0%, EBITDA growth at least +35% vs FY 2024.

### Portfolio
Key brands: Believe (core artist services), TuneCore (DIY distribution), Nuclear Blast (metal), Naïve (French indie label), Groove Attack (Germany — completed physical exit January 2026), AllPoints (US), Sentric (UK publishing, acquired March 2023). Technology: Backstage proprietary analytics platform, Be Odyssey Central Platform programme. Partnerships: YouTube Creator Music (September 2022), TikTok Artist Impact Programme (May 2023).

### Operations Update
Groove Attack (Germany) completed physical distribution exit effective January 2026 — digital-only transition confirmed. Premium Solutions upsell from TuneCore Accelerator: 890 conversions in January — monthly record. French rap roster placed 4 tracks in French Spotify top 20. Germany team pitched 3 electronic acts to Spotify DE Neue Musik Freitag for February.
