## Spotify — Policy and Feature Updates

### Minimum Stream Threshold for Royalties
**Date:** April 2024 (ongoing)
**What changed:** Tracks must reach 1,000 streams per year to qualify for
royalty payments. Approximately 60% of tracks on Spotify fall below this
threshold and are now demonetised. Revenue redistributed to higher-stream tracks.
Developed in partnership with major labels including Universal Music Group.
**Impact on artists:** Significant for early-stage self-releasing artists who
may have small but genuine fanbases. Artists earning small royalties now earn
nothing unless they cross the threshold.
**Relevance to Believe:** 🔴 RISK for TuneCore tier — reduces viability of
very early-stage artists and may increase churn from TuneCore. However,
creates urgency for TuneCore Accelerator to help artists cross the threshold,
which is an opportunity for upsell to Premium Solutions.
**Source:** https://www.unchainedmusic.io/blog-posts/spotifys-new-royalty-policy-may-leave-many-artists-unpaid

### Music Videos Official Launch
**Date:** 2025
**What changed:** Music videos exited beta and rolled out to 100+ global markets.
Artists can attach official videos and live performances to their tracks.
Listeners can switch between audio and video within the app.
**Impact on artists:** New content type to create and optimise. Video content
drives higher engagement and longer session times.
**Relevance to Believe:** 🟢 OPPORTUNITY — Believe's video distribution capabilities
(already part of Premium Solutions) become more strategically valuable as
Spotify's video layer grows.
**Source:** https://routenote.com/blog/spotify-for-artists-2025-a-recap-on-all-the-new-features/

### AI Content Policy Update
**Date:** 2025 (ongoing)
**What changed:** Spotify updated policy to protect artists from AI impersonation,
require AI disclosure labels, and implement automated fraud detection. Removed
75 million spam/fake tracks in 2024. Cracking down on vocal cloning.
**Impact on artists:** Genuine artists benefit from cleaner platform — less
competition from AI-generated content for editorial placements. Artists must
now disclose AI usage in their releases.
**Relevance to Believe:** 🟢 OPPORTUNITY — cleanup improves editorial signal
quality. Believe's artists (genuine, independent musicians) benefit as fraudulent
streams and fake content are removed from the royalty pool.
**Source:** https://imusician.pro/en/resources/blog/spotify-ai-policy-in-2025-artist-protection-transparency

### ChatGPT Integration
**Date:** October 2025
**What changed:** Spotify integrated into ChatGPT, available in 145 countries.
Users can receive personalised music recommendations via conversational AI
("calm indie songs for late-night focus").
**Impact on artists:** New discovery pathway via natural language description.
Mood-based and niche artists may benefit more than mainstream pop acts.
**Relevance to Believe:** 🟢 OPPORTUNITY — Believe's indie and niche roster
in DE/UK/FR are well-positioned for discovery via descriptive/emotional prompts.
**Source:** https://imusician.pro/en/resources/blog/how-spotify-changes-affect-the-music-industry

### Pre-Save Hub Launch
**Date:** Late 2024
**What changed:** Spotify launched dedicated pre-save hub showing top 10 most
pre-saved upcoming releases, updated daily. Artists and labels can track
pre-save counts as a release campaign metric.
**Impact on artists:** Pre-save campaigns now have a visibility component beyond
just the artist's own marketing. High pre-save counts create social proof.
**Relevance to Believe:** 🟢 OPPORTUNITY — Believe's Premium Solutions team
can now use pre-save count as a KPI for release campaigns and demonstrate ROI
to artists more clearly.
**Source:** https://rebelmusicdistribution.com/2025/09/30/spotify-removes-75-million-fake-tracks-in-2024-what-it-means-for-artists-in-2025/

## Apple Music — Policy and Feature Updates

### Dolby Atmos Royalty Bonus (Confirmed Ongoing)
**Date:** January 2024 (confirmed, still in effect 2025–2026)
**What changed:** Apple Music pays an approximately 10% royalty bonus on
streams played in Dolby Atmos. Atmos listening crossed 50% of Apple Music
streams in late 2024, now estimated at 65–75% of all Apple Music plays in 2026.
**Impact on artists:** Artists without Atmos masters are leaving 6–8% of their
Apple Music revenue uncollected. 12 million+ tracks now mastered in Dolby Atmos.
**Relevance to Believe:** 🟢 OPPORTUNITY — Believe can add Atmos mastering
guidance to Premium Solutions artist services. TuneCore Mastering tool (launched
Q2 2024) could be upgraded to include Atmos optimisation.
**Source:** https://www.chartlex.com/blog/streaming/apple-music-for-artists-complete-guide-2026

### Per-Stream Payout Rate (2025–2026)
**Date:** Ongoing
**Current rate:** $0.008–$0.010 per stream — approximately double Spotify's rate
**Mechanism:** No free/ad-supported tier means every stream is from a paying
subscriber. Royalty pool = subscription revenue minus Apple's cut.
**Relevance to Believe:** 🟢 OPPORTUNITY — For Believe's artists in markets
where Apple Music has 18–20% share (UK, Germany), optimising for Apple Music
editorial is high ROI. A release earning 100,000 streams split 70/30
Spotify/Apple Music earns more total revenue from 30,000 Apple Music streams
than from 70,000 Spotify streams.
**Source:** https://www.chartlex.com/blog/streaming/apple-music-for-artists-complete-guide-2026

## Deezer — Policy and Feature Updates

### AI Content Detection and Exclusion
**Date:** 2024–2025 (ongoing)
**What changed:** Deezer deployed detection tools to tag AI-generated content.
AI tracks are excluded from recommendation algorithms and limited from
editorial playlists. One of the first major platforms to act decisively on AI.
**Context:** Deezer reported receiving 30,000+ AI-generated tracks per day,
with approximately 70% of total streams linked to artificial plays.
**Impact on artists:** Genuine artists benefit from cleaner recommendations
and editorial playlists with less AI competition.
**Relevance to Believe:** 🟢 OPPORTUNITY — Believe's genuine artists benefit
from Deezer's clean-up. Believe's Deezer editorial relationship is an advantage
for French market placement.
**Source:** https://imusician.pro/en/resources/blog/spotify-ai-policy-in-2025-artist-protection-transparency

### Artist-Centric Payment Model Experiment
**Date:** Ongoing (2024–2025)
**What changed:** Deezer is experimenting with distributing each subscriber's
fee only to artists that subscriber actually listened to — rather than pooling
all revenue and distributing by total platform streams (pro-rata model).
**Impact on artists:** Artists with genuine, loyal fanbases earn more. Artists
with inflated or passive stream counts earn less. Directly rewards authentic
engagement.
**Relevance to Believe:** 🟢 OPPORTUNITY — Believe's roster of genuine
independent artists with real fanbases benefits under user-centric model.
Believe should monitor roll-out and advocate for wider adoption.
**Source:** https://www.prolificdaily.com/2025/08/highest-paying-music-streaming-platforms-2025.html

## TikTok — Music-Related Updates

### Artist Impact Program (Active)
**Date:** May 2023 onwards
**What:** Believe is a global distribution partner — artists can participate
in TikTok's Commercial Music Library and add trending songs.
**Relevance to Believe:** 🟢 OPPORTUNITY — Believe artists have preferential
access to TikTok's commercial music ecosystem.
**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Believe_Music

### Discovery Dominance (2024–2025)
**Fact:** 60% of Spotify streams for emerging artists now trace back to
TikTok discovery (MusicWatch 2024). TikTok is the primary discovery
mechanism for under-25 demographic across DE/UK/FR markets.
**Relevance to Believe:** 🟢 OPPORTUNITY AND 🟡 MONITOR — Believe should
ensure Premium Solutions includes TikTok content strategy. However, TikTok's
ongoing regulatory issues in various markets (potential bans, ownership disputes)
create platform risk for artists dependent on it.
