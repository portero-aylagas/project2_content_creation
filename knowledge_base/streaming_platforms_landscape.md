# Streaming Platforms Landscape

## Spotify

### Market Position
- Global market share: approximately 31% of global music streaming
- Monthly active users: 700 million+ (as of 2025)
- Paying subscribers: 281 million across 237 countries and territories
- Total royalties paid 2014–2024: over $10 billion
- Artists on platform: approximately 12 million
- Per-stream payout: $0.003–$0.005 (lowest of major paid platforms)

### Key Policy Changes (2024–2025)

**1,000-Stream Minimum Threshold (April 2024)**
Tracks must achieve 1,000 streams per year to qualify for royalty payments.
This effectively demonetises approximately 60% of tracks on the platform.
Revenue is redistributed to higher-stream tracks. Developed in partnership
with major labels. Impact on independent artists: significant — many early-stage
artists on TuneCore will fall below the threshold.
🔴 RISK for Believe's TuneCore tier — reduces viability of very early-stage
self-releasing artists.

**AI Content Policy (2025)**
Spotify updated its AI policy to protect artists from impersonation, combat
spam and fraud, and require transparency labelling for AI-generated content.
Removed over 75 million spam/fake tracks in 2024. Cracking down on vocal
cloning and impersonation. Requiring artists to disclose AI usage.
🟢 OPPORTUNITY — cleanup improves editorial playlist signal quality,
benefiting genuine independent artists on Believe's roster.

**Music Videos (2025)**
Music videos officially exited beta and rolled out to 100+ global markets.
Fans can switch between audio and video within the app. Bridges gap between
Spotify and YouTube for visual content.
🟢 OPPORTUNITY — adds a new content type that Believe can help artists
create and optimise for.

**ChatGPT Integration (October 2025)**
Spotify integrated into ChatGPT, allowing personalised music recommendations
via conversational AI across 145 countries. Creates new discovery pathway
based on emotional/descriptive prompts rather than genre tags.
🟢 OPPORTUNITY — niche and mood-based independent artists may benefit from
discovery via natural language ("calm indie songs for late-night focus").

**Pre-Save Hub (Late 2024)**
Spotify launched a dedicated hub showing top 10 most pre-saved upcoming releases,
updated daily. Labels and artists can track pre-save counts.
🟢 OPPORTUNITY — Believe can use this as a release campaign metric.

## Apple Music

### Market Position
- Global market share: approximately 12–15% of paid streaming subscribers
- Estimated subscribers: 95–110 million paying users worldwide (2026)
- Per-stream payout: $0.008–$0.010 — approximately double Spotify's rate
- No free/ad-supported tier at meaningful scale — every stream is from
  a paying subscriber
- Pays an additional ~10% royalty bonus on streams played in Dolby Atmos
- Atmos listening: crossed 50% of Apple Music streams in late 2024,
  estimated at 65–75% of Apple Music plays in 2026

### Key Policy Updates
- January 2024: Dolby Atmos royalty bonus policy confirmed and still in effect
- Pitching: one-shot submission per release via Apple Music for Artists app —
  artists get one chance per song to pitch editorial team
- Editorial playlists: New Music Daily, Today's Hits, A-List by genre
- Discovery: Shazam (Apple product), editorial playlists, personalised stations

### Relevance for Believe
Apple Music pays approximately double per stream vs Spotify. For Believe's
artists in DE/UK/FR — markets where Apple Music has 18–20% share among
premium listeners — optimising for Apple Music editorial is high ROI.
Dolby Atmos masters can increase Apple Music revenue by 6–8%.
🟢 OPPORTUNITY — Believe can add Atmos mastering guidance to artist services.

## Deezer

### Market Position
- Global market share: approximately 6–8% globally
- France market share: 28% — dramatically higher than global average
  (home market advantage as French company)
- Germany market share: approximately 8%
- Model: experimenting with artist-centric (user-centric) payment model —
  each subscriber's fee distributed only among artists they actually listened to
- This model benefits niche and indie artists with loyal fanbases vs
  pop artists who benefit from pro-rata pooling

### Key Policy Updates
- AI content: Deezer has deployed detection tools to tag AI-generated content,
  excludes AI tracks from recommendation algorithms, and limits their
  participation in editorial playlists — one of the first platforms to act
- Receives over 30,000 AI-generated tracks per day (reported)
- Approximately 70% of total streams linked to artificial plays (reported by Deezer)
- Artist-centric payment model experiments ongoing

### Relevance for Believe
Deezer's French home market position is one of Believe's key advantages.
Believe has preferential editorial relationships with Deezer that competitors
lack. The artist-centric payment model benefits Believe's artists who have
genuine, loyal fanbases rather than inflated stream counts.
🟢 OPPORTUNITY — Believe should ensure all Premium Solutions artists are
optimised for Deezer FR editorial specifically.

## YouTube Music

### Market Position
- Growing, particularly among 18–24 demographic in Germany
- YouTube (non-Music) remains critical for music video consumption
  and independent artist discovery globally
- Creator Music programme: Believe was among YouTube's independent
  partners when it launched September 2022
- TikTok Artist Impact Program: Believe is a global partner (May 2023)

### TikTok (Music Discovery)
- 60% of Spotify streams for emerging artists now trace back to TikTok
  discovery (MusicWatch 2024)
- Dominant discovery mechanism for under-25 audience across DE/UK/FR
- TikTok-to-streaming pipeline most developed in UK among European markets
- Believe's TikTok partnership (Artist Impact Program) allows artists
  to participate in TikTok's Commercial Music Library
