# Believe Company Profile

## Overview

Believe is a French digital music company founded in Paris in 2005 by Denis
Ladegaillerie. It is the world's largest independent digital music distributor,
operating across more than 50 countries with approximately 2,020 employees.
The company distributes music to over 200 streaming platforms and digital stores
worldwide, including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Deezer, Amazon Music,
and TikTok. Believe is not owned by a major label and operates as a genuinely
independent company, which is a core part of its brand identity and artist
proposition.

In April 2024, Believe was taken majority-private by a consortium including
founder Denis Ladegaillerie, investment firms TCV and EQT. By July 2025 the
consortium held 96.63% of Believe's share capital. Prior to privatisation,
Believe was listed on Euronext Paris (Ticker: BLV, ISIN: FR0014003FE9).

## Business Model

Believe operates a dual-platform model:

### Premium Solutions (Artist Services)
- Full-service distribution and marketing for established and growing artists
- Revenue-share model: Believe takes a percentage of digital sales and streams
- Includes: editorial playlist pitching, press outreach, paid social campaigns,
  video distribution, artist development, publishing administration
- Dedicated Artist Solutions manager per campaign
- Access to Believe's direct editorial relationships at major DSPs
- Target artist tier: 50,000+ monthly Spotify listeners

### Automated Solutions (TuneCore)
- Self-service DIY distribution platform for emerging/self-releasing artists
- Flat annual subscription fee, artists keep majority of royalties
- TuneCore Accelerator: flagship marketing program enrolling 450,000+
  self-releasing artists in 2024 — helps 1 in 5 advance to higher segment
  with median royalty earnings increasing fivefold
- Target artist tier: 0–50,000 monthly Spotify listeners

### Key Portfolio Brands
- Believe (core artist services)
- TuneCore (DIY distribution)
- Nuclear Blast (metal label)
- Naïve (French independent label)
- Groove Attack (German distribution)
- AllPoints (US label services)
- Sentric (music publishing administration, acquired March 2023)
- Ishtar and Byond (regional labels)

## Geographic Presence and Key Markets

### Core European Markets
- **France**: Home market, strong domestic rap, electronic, and pop roster.
  Revenue grew 10.3% in FY 2024 despite adverse Q4 for non-digital sales.
  Home to Sentric publishing operation.
- **Germany**: Believe's strategy is to exit physical distribution contracts
  and focus on digital. Revenue grew just 0.4% YoY in FY 2024 but digital
  trajectory returned to positive. Groove Attack label based in Cologne.
- **UK**: Growth positively uplifted by Sentric integration. Strong position
  in publishing administration. Sea Girls (UK band) among roster highlights.

### Growth Markets
- Americas: 18% revenue growth in FY 2024
- Eastern Europe: strong growth, Turkey confirmed leadership
- Spain: strong growth recorded in FY 2024
- India: acquired White Hill Music (Punjabi catalog) December 2023
- Japan: launched PlayCode imprint (hip-hop) early 2024
- Indonesia: launched Krumulo imprint (Java Pop) 2024

### Notable Recent Acquisitions
- Sentric (music publishing, UK) — March 2023
- White Hill Music (Punjabi catalog, India) — December 2023
- Global Records stake (25%, Romania) — H1 2024
- Turkish label DMC — completed 2024

## Technology Platform

Believe operates a central technology platform it refers to as the "Central
Platform". In 2024 it launched 'Be Odyssey', a programme to further optimise
the platform's technological capabilities. Key technology initiatives include:

- **Backstage**: Believe's proprietary artist analytics platform — real-time
  streaming data, audience demographics, playlist tracking, competitor benchmarking
- **TuneCore Accelerator**: AI-assisted marketing program for self-releasing
  artists, using catalog optimisation technology
- **TuneCore Mastering**: AI mastering tool added Q2 2024 to help artists
  improve sound quality before release
- AI partnerships: Believe explored AI partnerships from April 2023, focusing
  on marketing and promotion applications
- TikTok Artist Impact Program: Believe among global partners since May 2023
